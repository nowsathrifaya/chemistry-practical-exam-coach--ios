Chemistry Practical Exam Coach — Build v5
=========================================

Goal: adaptive mastery and exam-readiness build on top of v4.

Implemented:
1. AdaptiveCoach engine converts persistent Attempt history into targeted next actions.
2. Today's Focus card added to Home and Progress.
3. Recommendations route directly to QA, calculations, ACE topic/skill, simulations,
   apparatus and graph practice.
4. Advanced QA, water-of-crystallisation and salt-preparation simulations now record
   completed results into SwiftData attempt history.
5. Fixed the water-of-crystallisation mass-of-water calculation bug.
6. Replaced the minimal orphaned MockPracticalView with a dedicated 5-question,
   10-mark Paper 3-style practical mock and persistent result recording.
7. ACE Leitner state remains persisted with UserDefaults per curriculum; no unnecessary
   SwiftData migration was introduced.
8. Version updated to 1.2.0 (build 3).

Design principle:
Attempt -> detect weak area -> recommend focused practice -> retest.

Known next improvements:
- Per-question QA subskill analytics and targeted chloride/cation retests.
- Calculation practice self-marking and attempt recording.
- Graph Coach/Apparatus Trainer content expansion.
- Haptics/sound integration for new advanced simulations.
- Optional migration of Leitner state to SwiftData if cross-device sync becomes required.
