Chemistry Practical Exam Coach — Build v4 Specification
=====================================================

This document specifies all changes made in the v4 integration + quality build,
based on the v3 audit and the user's 9-step implementation plan.

The guiding principle: everything is connected. No orphaned views, no template
questions, no disconnected data. Every feature feeds the progress system.

3. FULL QA LABORATORY (Step 3)
------------------------------

QualitativeAnalysisLab.swift (NEW — 536 lines)
  - Multi-step workflow: select unknown → add reagent → observe →
    add excess → conclude
  - 15 unknown samples including:
    * Cation-only: Cu²⁺, Fe²⁺, Fe³⁺, Zn²⁺, Al³⁺, Ca²⁺, NH₄⁺
    * Anion-only: Cl⁻, SO₄²⁻, CO₃²⁻, NO₃⁻, I⁻, Br⁻
    * Mixed salts: CuCl₂, FeSO₄
  - 10 available reagents: NaOH (aqueous), NaOH (warm), NH₃, HNO₃+AgNO₃,
    HNO₃+Ba(NO₃)₂, dilute HCl, limewater, damp red litmus,
    NaOH+Al foil, flame test
  - 21 observation options covering all standard QA observations
  - Step indicator showing progress through the 5-step workflow
  - Test log recording all performed tests with observations and inferences
  - Excess reagent support (e.g., NH₃ dissolves Zn²⁺ precipitate but not Al³⁺)
  - Self-assessment conclusion with immediate feedback
  - Exam tips referencing SEAB Paper 3 QA notes

7. WATER OF CRYSTALLISATION + SALT PREPARATION (Step 7)
-------------------------------------------------------

WaterOfCrystallisationSim.swift (NEW — 313 lines)
  - Interactive simulation for determining x in hydrated salts
  - 4 salt options: CuSO₄·5H₂O, MgSO₄·7H₂O, Na₂CO₃·10H₂O, FeSO₄·7H₂O
  - 3-step workflow: select salt → heat to constant mass → calculate x
  - Progressive mass loss with each heating (3 heatings to constant mass)
  - Full calculation display: mass of water, moles of salt, moles of water,
    mole ratio
  - Student enters answer, gets immediate feedback with worked solution
  - Exam tips about gentle heating and constant mass technique

SaltPreparationSim.swift (NEW — 396 lines)
  - Decision-tree simulation for salt preparation
  - 6 salts covering all 3 methods:
    * Precipitation: PbSO₄, CaCO₃
    * Excess insoluble base: CuSO₄, MgCl₂
    * Titration: NaCl, KNO₃
  - 4-step workflow: select salt → identify solubility → choose method →
    execute procedure
  - Each salt has 5-step procedure with correct sequence
  - Progress log recording all decisions and steps
  - Exam tips specific to each salt and method
  - Decision guidance: solubility check → SPA check → method selection
